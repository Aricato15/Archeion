# Plan: Example-Project

## Phase 1: the catalogue and the cart (2026-11-01 to 2026-11-20)
- Goal: a reader can find books and fill a cart.
- Catalogue with search, the SQLite database, the cart.

## Phase 2: checkout (2026-11-21 to 2026-12-12)
- Goal: a reader can pay and get a receipt.
- Checkout and the hosted payment page, email confirmations, stock levels, the shipping carriers.

## Phase 3: extras (2027-01-11 to 2027-01-29)
- Goal: small things that make the shop nicer, only if readers ask.
- Wishlist, gift wrapping.
